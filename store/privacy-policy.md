# Broxy Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 August 2026

Broxy is a local proxy switcher browser extension. It routes only the websites you choose through a proxy that **you** run on your computer.

## Data Broxy stores

Broxy stores the following in the browser’s local storage (`chrome.storage.local`):

- whether the proxy switch is on or off
- the proxy protocol, host, and port you enter
- the list of domains you add

This data never leaves your browser. Broxy does not create an account, does not sync settings to a server, and does not use analytics, ads, or crash reporting.

## Data Broxy does not collect

Broxy does not collect or transmit:

- browsing history
- cookies or page content
- names, emails, or other personal identifiers
- proxy usernames or passwords (the browser’s own dialog handles those)

## Permissions

- **proxy** — apply or clear a PAC script so listed sites use your local proxy
- **storage** — save your settings on this device
- **activeTab** — read the current tab’s hostname when you open the popup, so you can add or remove that site
